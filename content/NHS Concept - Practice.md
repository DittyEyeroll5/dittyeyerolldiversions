In the UK NHS, they use a tiered referral network for accessing healthcare. People who get sick go first to their [[NHS Concept - GP|General Practitioners]] - general physicians who then evaluate patients and decide if they should refer them to specialists.

The clinics where GPs work are called a **Practice** hence the special term. These Practices are registered with the UK NHS and are thus included in OpenEyes as a [[OpenEyes Features|feature]].
